About this cluster

Classic British and American cinema -- Chaplin, Hitchcock, David Lean, the Archers -- spanning the studio era on both sides of the Atlantic, unified by an English-language star system whose actors crossed freely between UK and US productions. Nearly as densely interconnected as European Art Cinema -- shown here as degree bands rather than an edge graph for the same reason.
