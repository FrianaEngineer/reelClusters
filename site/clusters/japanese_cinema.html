About this cluster

The second-largest cluster and the most densely interconnected: classical Japanese studio cinema across Ozu, Kurosawa, Naruse, Kinoshita, and Kobayashi, whose stock-company acting culture means the same faces recur across hundreds of films from different directors and studios. Like European Art Cinema, too dense to draw as an edge graph -- shown here as degree bands instead.
