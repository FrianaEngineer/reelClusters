About this cluster

This cluster is built around Satyajit Ray's own filmography, the towering figure of Bengali and Indian art cinema in the Collection, along with Ritwik Ghatak's Bengali cinema and a handful of other Indian and Indian-adjacent films connected in by shared cast.

The cluster holds 18 films spanning 1955 to 1994, 15 of them Ray's own, from Apur Sansar and Devi in the early 1960s through later work like The Home and the World in the 1980s. It's small enough that it holds together almost entirely on the strength of Ray's own recurring collaborators, rather than a broader movement or national scene.

Shown as a shared-actor network of individual films, rather than grouped by connection count, given its small size. Devi is the clear hub with 14 connections, followed by Apur Sansar and The Elephant God at 9 each. Hover a film for its year and connection count, or click through to watch it on Criterion.
