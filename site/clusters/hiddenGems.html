Key Findings

Self-containment is near-universal. 33 of 36 communities (92%) have a 100% internal ratio -- no shared actors with any other community.

Director filmographies dominate. 11 of the 13 profiled (3+ film) communities are majority-led by a single director's own filmography.

National cinema boundaries hold for many. 11 of the 13 profiled communities draw every film with recorded country data from a single country.
